# Scenario summary

The executable corpus is defined in `rules.SCENARIOS` and validated by `tests/test_pipeline.py`. It contains the PDF Arena Mode cases CS-01 through CS-20, covering insider trading, spoofing, suitability, AML structuring, information barriers, wash trading, regulatory change, misleading marketing, sanctions, front-running, privacy, concentration, off-channel records, late trading, best execution, research conflicts, elder exploitation, a legitimate block-trade false positive, jurisdiction conflict, and model drift. Expected outcomes are asserted at every run; source labels and citations are retained in generated reports.
